📖 How to use the Recipe Planner

📋 Recipes

  • Tap a card to add it to your weekly plan — tap again to remove it.
  • Filter by Diet, Type, Time, and Nutrition. The search bar finds recipes by name or subtitle.
  • Use the History filter to show only recipes you've never tried, tried before, or cooked 3+ times.
  • 👨‍🍳 view step-by-step cooking instructions · ✏ edit any recipe · 🗃 archive to hide it from the grid.
  • Use + Add Recipe to create your own custom meals.

❤ Favourites

  • Tap the ♡ heart on any recipe card to mark it as a favourite — it turns red. Tap again to remove it.
  • Tap ❤ Favourites in the filter bar to show only your favourited recipes.
  • Favourites are saved to your profile and sync across devices when signed in.

🗓 Weekly Plan

  • Shows all your selected meals. Adjust serving sizes with the dropdown on each card.
  • Tap × on a meal to remove it from the plan without leaving this tab.
  • Save this week saves a snapshot to Plan History so you can revisit it later.
  • Mark week as cooked logs a cook count on every meal in the plan — used by the History filter in the Recipes tab.

🛒 Shopping List

  • Auto-generated from your selected meals and serving sizes. Common staples (salt, oil, water) are hidden.
  • Tap an item to mark it as got (strikethrough). Tap the amount to edit it inline.
  • 🛒 next to each item opens a Sainsbury's search for that ingredient.
  • Open Fresh and Open Pantry open a Sainsbury's search tab for every unticked item in that section at once — ticked items are skipped.
  • Tip: log into Sainsbury's first before using Open Fresh/Pantry so items go straight into your trolley.
  • Use Print to print the list or save as PDF — ticked items are hidden from the printout.

📚 Plan History

  • Each saved week appears as a card showing the meals and serving sizes.
  • Tap Load Plan to restore that week's meals into your current weekly plan.
  • Share a saved plan via the Gmail button — recipients can import it into their own planner.
  • Tap × on a history card to permanently delete that saved week.

👤 Your Account

  • Sign in with Google (top of the page) to save your data across devices.
  • Your weekly plan, favourites, cook history, custom recipes, and archived recipes all sync automatically when signed in.
